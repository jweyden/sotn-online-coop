# Castlevania: Symphony of the Night - Online Co-op

Up to three players, each on their own computer, one castle. Play as Alucard, Richter
or Maria, from the PlayStation, PSP and Saturn versions of the game.

## Play

1. Download **sotn-coop-online.zip** from [Releases](../../releases) and unzip it.
2. Put your own game files in its three folders:
   - `1 - PlayStation game`: *Castlevania: Symphony of the Night*, US (SLUS-00067), `.cue` + `.bin`
   - `2 - PSP game`: *Castlevania: The Dracula X Chronicles*, Europe (ULES-00841), `.iso`
   - `3 - Saturn game`: *Akumajou Dracula X: Gekka no Yasoukyoku*, Japan (T-9527G), `.cue` + `.bin`
3. Run **Setup** (Mac or Windows). It builds the game and every character from your games.
4. Run **Play**, then press Triangle on the title screen to host or join.

Everything else, step by step: [READ ME FIRST.txt](READ%20ME%20FIRST.txt).
